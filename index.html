Privacy Policy for Voice Flashlight

Last updated: October 2026

Voice Flashlight ("the App", "we", "our") is developed by Denis Onistsenko. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data when you use it.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. All voice recognition and flashlight processing happens entirely on your device using offline models. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. To deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and report ad performance, AdMob and its third-party vendors may collect and use:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • IP address (used to determine approximate location)
  • Technical device specifications (device model, OS version, screen size, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, conversion events)

These technologies (cookies, Advertising IDs, or similar) are used by third-party vendors to serve ads. You can learn more and manage your ad preferences at:

  • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Ads Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Opt out of personalized ads: https://optout.aboutads.info

On Android, you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

The App uses Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases (e.g., the "Remove Ads" purchase). All payment information is handled directly by Google. We do not have access to your payment details.

See Google Play's Terms of Service: https://play.google.com/intl/en/about/play-terms/

3. Permissions We Use

  • Camera / Flashlight: Used to control your device's LED flashlight. No photos or videos are captured.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used for offline voice command recognition. Audio is processed on-device and immediately discarded.
  • Notifications: Used to show when background voice listening is active (foreground service).
  • Internet: Used only to load advertisements via AdMob and process in-app purchases via Google Play.

4. User Data Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because all voice recognition and flashlight data are processed locally on your device, we do not store any personal user data on external servers. This means there is no data on our side to access, correct, or delete.

You can stop all local data processing at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the application, or
  • Revoking permissions in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Voice Flashlight → Permissions)

For ad-related data collected by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, opt-out of personalization) through Google's privacy tools linked in Section 2 above.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR/CCPA regarding personal data. Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, you should direct such requests to Google (as the ad provider) using the links above, or contact us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). The App is not directed at children. If you believe a child has used the App and provided data through AdMob, please contact us.

6. Data Retention

We do not retain any user data on external servers. Ad-related data retention is managed by Google AdMob according to Google's privacy policy.

7. Security

All app functionality runs locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal data on our infrastructure. Third-party services (AdMob, Google Play Billing) use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
