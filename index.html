Privacy Policy for Voice Flashlight

Last updated: October 2026

Voice Flashlight ("the App") is developed by Denis Onistsenko. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All voice recognition is processed entirely on your device using offline models. No audio is ever sent to any server.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The App uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Google processes all payment information. We do not have access to your payment details.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Used to control the device flashlight
  • Microphone: Used for offline voice command recognition
  • Notifications: Used to show when background listening is active

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
